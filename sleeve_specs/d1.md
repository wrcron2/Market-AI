# D1 sleeve — pinned specification

Intraday opening-range-breakout sleeve. Status: **parked** — plan §7 option B
is the default and no evidence yet overrides it. No capital is allocated; the
code path is kept. The G5 gate (week 16) decides between re-cut (A), stay
parked (B), and drop (C), with fortress output in hand.

## Why parked (the arithmetic, plan §7)

At 1–2 trades/day on a $1,500 sleeve, friction is **14–51% of the sleeve per
year**, and below ~$45/share friction alone exceeds the entire stylized ORB
edge (8.5 bps). Parking costs nothing; trading costs the friction.

## Pinned parameters (plan §8)

- **Status: parked** (explicit default; not an open question).
- **Price band if re-cut: ≥$80 liquid names only** (plan §7 option A / §8) —
  the penny-spread asymmetry below that price is what makes the cheap names
  untradeable at this size. Band floor 80.0, no cap, recorded in
  `fortress/hurdles.yaml` as `price_band_usd_if_recut`.
- **Gross hurdle: > 10 bps per trade** (`fortress/hurdles.yaml`); a re-cut D1
  restates it to **~6 bps gross** for the ~50 high-conviction trades/yr
  variant (§7 option A).
- **Sleeve notional: $1,500** — unchanged from the plan; whole shares, round
  down; cash buffer ≥ 1 share of the most expensive candidate in the
  ≥$80 band at the time of any re-cut.
- **Cadence:** intraday, 1–2 trades/day at most; the re-cut variant targets
  ~50 trades/yr.

## Revival protocol (non-negotiable if G5 chooses A or otherwise revives)

1. Minimum **100 paper trades** at the intended cadence.
2. Realized friction tracked against the shared cost model; go-live only if
   gross expectancy clears the written hurdle AND realized friction lands
   inside model bands.
3. The pre-registration (`preregistration_template.md`) must state in
   advance that even a clean 100-trade paper run **cannot confirm** a ~10 bps
   edge — it can only fail to reject it (plan §7 power analysis:
   distinguishing 8 from 12 bps takes ~2,400 trades). Any live D1 starts at
   toy size, buying information, not returns.
4. IBKR Rule 4210 implementation status for the account (open question Q2)
   must be answered before any intraday margin reliance.
