# M2 sleeve — pinned specification

Swing sleeve. Status: pre-paper — its gate is the plan's G3 (4 clean paper
weeks, M1+M2, realized friction inside model bands). No capital is allocated
before that gate.

## Pinned parameters (plan §8)

- **Hold window: the bounded range [3, 10] trading days.** §8 allows an
  explicit bounded range when its cost consequence is stated; the pin rule is
  that the fortress evaluates **both endpoints** at the M2 gate and the
  single value adopted is whichever clears the hurdles (Rule A and Rule B,
  same as M1) with the higher net expectancy. Until that run, no value inside
  the range is claimed.
- **Cost consequence of the range** (computed with the shared cost model,
  `fortress/cost_model.py`; 5 × $1,000 positions, whole shares round down,
  worst case = full both-sides sleeve turnover once per hold window):

  | Execution price | Shares/position | Cost per side | Full rotation (both sides) | 3-day holds (84 rot/yr) | 10-day holds (25 rot/yr) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | $20 (band floor) | 50 | $1.010 | $10.10 = 20.2 bps of sleeve | 16.97%/yr | 5.09%/yr |
  | $60 (mid-band anchor) | 16 | $0.689 | $6.89 = 13.8 bps of sleeve | 11.58%/yr | 3.47%/yr |
  | $150 (band cap) | 6 | $0.549 | $5.49 = 11.0 bps of sleeve | 9.23%/yr | 2.77%/yr |

  So the window choice is worth **~6–12 percentage points of friction drag
  per year** at full turnover — the 3-day endpoint must show materially more
  gross edge than the 10-day endpoint to survive Rule B. Actual turnover will
  be below the full-turnover bound (membership-change-only trading); the
  bound is the conservative planning number.
- **Price band: $20–150** — same rationale as M1 (whole-share granularity at
  $640–1,070 sizing).
- **Sizing:** $5,000 sleeve notional; $1,000 per position target; **whole
  shares, round down**; residual cash earns 0% in the model.
- **Cash buffer: ≥ $150 un-deployed** (one share of the most expensive
  in-band candidate, plan §8).
- **Universe:** S&P 100 point-in-time from the §3 data layer — same source
  and provenance rules as M1.

## Hurdles (pre-registered, `fortress/hurdles.yaml`)

- Rule A: bootstrap 95% CI lower bound of annualized net expectancy > 0, at
  the pinned cadence (each endpoint evaluated separately).
- Rule B: annualized net expectancy > 2 × annualized friction drag.

## Pre-registration

The endpoint evaluation runs under a filed `preregistration_template.md`
before the M2 fortress run; expected CI width is computed from assumed
per-hold-period volatility and the trade count at each endpoint.
