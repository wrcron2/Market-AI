# M1 sleeve — pinned specification

Cross-sectional momentum rotation. Status: **go** (Item Zero verdict
2026-08-03, reproduced by the fortress at the G1 gate). Paper trading at the
G3 gate; live pilot ($1,100) only at G4 under the plan §9 kill criteria.

## Pinned parameters (plan §8)

- **Rotation cadence: monthly.** Pinned by the fortress verdict — the higher
  net expectancy with CI lower bound above zero:
  - monthly: net **70.98%**/yr, CI95 [32.31, 103.99], friction drag
    **0.72%**/yr — both hurdles pass with margin;
  - weekly: net 59.61%/yr, CI95 [1.21, 119.73], drag 1.49%/yr — passes but
    its CI lower bound barely clears zero, so it is not the pin.
  - Expected friction drag of the pinned cadence, written next to its
    expectancy as §8 requires: **0.72%/yr modeled**, measured on
    membership-change-only turnover of ~92.7% of sleeve per rotation
    (both sides).
- **Price band: $20–150** (raw close on the signal day). Whole-share rounding
  granularity dominates outside this band at $640–1,070 position sizes.
- **Sizing:** sleeve notional $5,000 (no compounding in the model); target
  $1,000 per position; **whole shares, round down**; residual cash earns 0%
  in the expectancy model. Rounding strands 5–20% of the sleeve in cash at
  $60–150 names — that drag is inside the model, not on top of it.
- **Cash buffer: ≥ $150 un-deployed** (one share of the most expensive
  in-band candidate, the plan §8 rule), so a sell-then-buy rotation can
  always round down to whole shares without blocking an exit.
- **Universe:** S&P 100, point-in-time membership from the §3 data layer
  (`data_layer/`), delistings retained; symbol continuity per `symbol_map`.
- **Signal (frozen, `item_zero_decision_rule.md` §2):** 90-trading-day total
  return on split-and-dividend-adjusted closes; top 5 band-eligible names;
  equal target weight; membership-change-only trading (persistent names
  untouched); no stop-loss, no overlays, no parameter sweeps.
- **Execution assumption:** rebalance-day raw open; signals and returns on
  adjusted prices, share counts and notionals on raw prices (the §3
  adjustment boundary).
